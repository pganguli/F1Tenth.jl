API

The layers mirror the Python package: the gym environment, the simulator and its cars, then the dynamics, collision and LiDAR models.

Environment

F1Tenth.F110Env — Type
F110Env(; seed=12345, map="vegas", map_ext=".png", params=VehicleParams(), num_agents=2,
          timestep=0.01, ego_idx=1, integrator=Integrator.RK4, lidar_dist=nothing, lidar_sweep=true)

The F1TENTH gym environment, with the keyword arguments of Python's gym.make('f110_gym:f110-v0', **kwargs):

  • seed: seed of the LiDAR noise
  • map: "berlin", "skirk", "levine", "vegas", a track from f1tenth_racetracks such as "Austin" or "Spa" (downloaded on first use; see F1Tenth.RACETRACKS), or the path of a map yaml file without its extension
  • map_ext: extension of the map image, such as ".png" (built-in maps default to their own)
  • params: a VehicleParams or a dict with all of its keys
  • num_agents: number of cars
  • timestep: physics time step in seconds
  • ego_idx: index of the ego car (1-based)
  • integrator: Integrator.RK4 or Integrator.Euler
  • lidar_dist: how far the LiDAR sits ahead of the car's center of gravity, the point the pose gives (Python's docstring says the rear axle, but its code also measures from the pose); by default params.lidar_x - params.lr, where NUC5 carries it (0.143 m)
  • lidar_sweep (not in Python): take each LiDAR beam from the pose at its own time in the sweep, as the real sensor does (true), or the whole scan from the pose at its first beam (false)

Drive it with reset!, step! and render!.

F1Tenth.step! — Function
step!(sim::Simulator, control_inputs) -> Observation

Advance every car one step. control_inputs is num_agents × 2: desired steering angle in column 1, desired speed in column 2.

source
step!(env::F110Env, action) -> (obs, reward, done, info)

Advance one time step. action is num_agents × 2: desired steering angle in column 1, desired speed in column 2. reward is the time step; done is true once the ego car crashes or every car has done two laps; info.checkpoint_done[i] is true once car i has.

The returned Observation and info are overwritten by the next call.

F1Tenth.reset! — Function
reset!(car::RaceCar, pose)

Put the car at rest at pose = (x, y, θ), reset its actuator chain, clock and collision flag, and restart its scan noise from seed. With a map set, it takes a first scan there (the car is at rest, so the sweep doesn't matter); the next sweep starts one scan period later.

source
reset!(sim::Simulator, poses)

Put car i at row i of poses (num_agents × 3: x, y, θ).

source
reset!(env::F110Env, poses) -> (obs, reward, done, info)

Start a new episode with car i at row i of poses (num_agents × 3: x, y, θ). As in Python, this takes one zero-input step and returns its result.

F1Tenth.render! — Function
render!(env; mode=:human)

Draw the environment in a window (needs using GLMakie). Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. :human paces the window to real time: each frame waits until the wall clock catches up with the sim clock (Python just waits 5 ms per frame). If drawing falls behind by more than 0.1 s, or after a reset, the clocks are lined up again rather than rushed. :human_fast never waits.

F1Tenth.Observation — Type
Observation

What step! and reset! return: the entries of Python's observation dict (ego_idx, scans, poses_x, poses_y, poses_theta, linear_vels_x, linear_vels_y, ang_vels_z, collisions, lap_times, lap_counts). Read them as obs.poses_x, obs[:poses_x] or obs["poses_x"].

scan_times (not in Python) is the time since reset of the first beam of each car's scan in scans, as ROS stamps a scan.

The arrays are reused: each step overwrites them. copy(obs) keeps a snapshot.

Simulator(params, num_agents, seed; time_step=0.01, ego_idx=1,

F1Tenth.RaceCar — Type
RaceCar(params, seed; is_ego=false, time_step=0.01, num_beams=1081, fov=3π/2,
        integrator=Integrator.Euler, lidar_dist=params.lidar_x - params.lr, lidar_sweep=true)

One vehicle: the vehicle model, its low-level actuator chain (command limiter, steering servo, VESC speed loop; see F1Tenth.actuate!) and a LiDAR scanning at params.scan_rate. With lidar_sweep each beam is taken from the pose at its own time in the sweep, as the real sensor does; without, the whole scan is taken from the pose at its first beam. state is (x, y, steer_angle, vel, yaw_angle, yaw_rate, slip_angle). Scan noise is drawn from scan_rng; set it to nothing for noise-free scans (as rng=None in Python).

Cars made by a Simulator share its scan simulator and beam tables, like the class attributes of Python's RaceCar.

body_hits_wall(map, x, y, θ, length, width) -> Bool

Whether a car body, the length × width rectangle centered at (x, y) with heading θ, overlaps a wall cell of map (pixel value <= 128). Exact for the grid: the rectangle is tested against each nearby wall cell's square. Cells off the map are free.

F1Tenth.VehicleParams — Type
VehicleParams(; kwargs...)
VehicleParams(d::AbstractDict)

Parameters of one car: the vehicle model (see vehicle_dynamics_st) and its low-level actuator chain (see RaceCar). Every default is a published value for ForzaETH's NUC5 F1TENTH car, or, where ForzaETH publish none, a published default of the hardware it runs. "Race stack" below is ForzaETH/race_stack, branch ros2-humble, where NUC5's configuration lives.

Tires (race stack, stack_master/config/NUC5/pacejka/dubi/default.yaml: ForzaETH's tire identification of NUC5 on their "dubi" floor, with friction_coeff: 1.0)

  • mu = 1.0: friction scale on every tire force; 1 is the identification floor
  • Bf = 4.142, Cf = 0.4375, Df = 2.6476, Ef = 0.2332 and Br = 4.8721, Cr = 2.1651, Dr = 1.057, Er = 1.692: front and rear Magic Formula coefficients. The lateral force on an axle is mu * D * Fz * sin(C * atan(B*α - E*(B*α - atan(B*α)))), so D bounds the friction coefficient. The rear force peaks at 1.00 times the load at 0.25 rad of slip; with Er > 1 it then falls, and reverses past 0.63 rad, an artifact of the fit (the car is spinning by then). The front builds slowly: 0.45, 0.77 and 1.11 times the load at 0.1, 0.2 and 0.4 rad.

Body (race stack, stack_master/config/NUC5/car_model.yaml)

  • lf = 0.130, lr = 0.127: center of gravity to front/rear axle (m)
  • h = 0.074: height of the center of gravity (m)
  • m = 2.783: mass (kg)
  • I = 0.0627: yaw moment of inertia (kg m²)

Steering hardware (race stack, stack_master/config/NUC5/car_model.yaml)

  • s_min = -0.4189, s_max = 0.4189: mechanical steering angle limits (rad)
  • sv_min = -3.2, sv_max = 3.2: steering velocity limits (rad/s)
  • tau_steer = 0.15779476: time constant of the servo, which follows its command as a first-order lag (s)

Command limiter (ForzaETH's actuation_manager, in ForzaETH/f1tenth_system, with NUC5's limits from the race stack, stack_master/config/NUC5/vesc.yaml)

  • command_rate = 75.0: how often it passes a command on (Hz)
  • steer_cmd_min = -0.3271441202, steer_cmd_max = 0.2917771883: steering command range (rad)
  • steer_rate = 2.4615384615: steering command slew limit (rad/s)
  • speed_cmd_min = -14.375, speed_cmd_max = 14.375: speed command range (m/s)
  • accel_cmd = 2.5, decel_cmd = 2.5: how fast the speed command may rise and fall (m/s²)

Motor controller (a VESC running its firmware's speed control, as vedderb/bldc implements it). ForzaETH don't publish their VESC settings, so all of these but erpm_gain come from one published configuration of an F1TENTH car's VESC: vesc1_apr9 (vesc/config/vesc1_apr9/vesc_mcconf.xml in TeoIlie/F1TENTH_System). It is the only published one for the stock F1TENTH motor (the motor of the stock f1tenth_original configuration, detected again) on firmware that can run ForzaETH's commands: braking in speed control and ERPM up to 100,000. Its speed loop settings are the firmware's defaults but the derivative filter.

  • erpm_gain = 4800.0: electrical RPM of the motor per m/s (race stack, NUC5's vesc.yaml, speed_to_erpm_gain)
  • erpm_ramp = 25000.0: speed setpoint ramp (ERPM/s)
  • vesc_kp = 0.004, vesc_ki = 0.004, vesc_kd = 0.0001, vesc_kd_filter = 0.5: speed PID gains and derivative filter
  • vesc_min_erpm = 900.0: below this setpoint the speed loop stops and the firmware holds the duty cycle at zero, shorting the windings (ERPM)
  • vesc_rate = 1000.0: speed loop rate (Hz)
  • current_max = 65.0: motor current limit (A)
  • flux_linkage = 0.000755263, motor_resistance = 0.006, motor_inductance = 8.6e-7: the motor's flux linkage (Wb), winding resistance (Ω) and inductance (H), as the VESC's motor detection measured them

LiDAR (the Hokuyo UST-10LX the stock F1TENTH stack drives with urg_node; Hokuyo's specification C-42-04077)

  • scan_rate = 40.0: scans per second (25 ms per revolution at 2400 rpm); the car sees the latest scan until the next one
  • scan_noise = 0.03: standard deviation of the range noise (m); the datasheet's repeatability bound, σ < 30 mm (white target, factory conditions), the only figure it publishes
  • scan_range = 10.0: detection range (m): the specification's 0.06 to 10 m on white Kent sheet (0.06 to 4 m at 10 % diffuse reflectance; set 4.0 for dark walls; 30 m at most). A beam that reaches no wall within it reads 65.533 m, and one closer than 21 mm reads 0.004 m: the error outputs the specification lists (65533 and 4, in mm). Noise is added only to valid distances
  • lidar_x = 0.27: how far the LiDAR sits ahead of the rear axle (m): race stack, NUC5's static_transforms.yaml, base_link (the rear axle's center) to laser. The environment's lidar_dist, measured from the center of gravity, defaults to lidar_x - lr

Resistance (off: no identified values for an F1TENTH car have been published; set them from a coast-down test of your own car)

  • C_roll = 0.0: rolling resistance coefficient (force C_roll * m * g)
  • CdA = 0.0: drag coefficient times frontal area (m², force ½ ρ CdA v²)

Body size for collisions and drawing

  • width = 0.228: race stack, NUC5's car_model.yaml (width_m) (m)
  • length = 0.58: ForzaETH's f1tenth_gym fork default; NUC5's configuration gives none (m)

A dict (String or Symbol keys) must hold every key; extra keys are ignored.

Dynamics

F1Tenth.vehicle_dynamics_st — Function
vehicle_dynamics_st(x, u_init, params::VehicleParams) -> SVector{7}

Dynamic single-track model with Magic Formula tires, a friction ellipse and an open-differential 4WD drivetrain. State x = (x, y, steering angle, velocity, yaw, yaw rate, slip angle), input u_init = (steering velocity, drive acceleration). The steering velocity is clamped to the steering limits; the drive acceleration is the motor's force over the mass, before the tires limit it. Below 0.5 m/s it switches to the kinematic model.

The lateral force on each axle is mu * D * Fz * sin(C * atan(B*α - E*(B*α - atan(B*α)))), scaled by sqrt(1 - (Fx/Fmax)^2) for the grip the drive force Fx uses. The body accelerations are ForzaETH's single-track model (their EKF and their f1tenth_gym fork), with the front drive force added:

v̇x = (Fxr + Fxf cos δ - Fyf sin δ - Fres) / m + vy ψ̇
v̇y = (Fyr + Fxf sin δ + Fyf cos δ) / m - vx ψ̇
ψ̈  = (lf (Fyf cos δ + Fxf sin δ) - lr Fyr) / I

where Fres is rolling resistance plus aerodynamic drag. See VehicleParams for where the parameters come from.

F1Tenth.drive_force_per_amp — Function
drive_force_per_amp(params) -> Float64

Drive force on the car per amp of motor current (N/A). A permanent-magnet motor under field-oriented control makes torque 1.5 * pole_pairs * flux_linkage * current; through the gearing, force times car speed equals torque times motor speed, and the motor's electrical speed per m/s is erpm_gain * 2π/60, so the pole pairs cancel: 1.5 * flux_linkage * erpm_gain * 2π/60. Drivetrain losses are not modeled.

source
F1Tenth.actuate! — Function
actuate!(car::RaceCar, steer, speed)

Advance car one time step under the planner command (steer, speed): the command limiter ticks at command_rate, the VESC speed loop and the steering servo run at vesc_rate, and the vehicle model is integrated over each of those sub-steps with the car's integrator. After each sub-step the body is checked against the walls (F1Tenth.body_hits_wall); on a hit the car stops where it is, keeping its heading, for the rest of the step. LiDAR sweeps start on the same sub-steps, and the car's pose is recorded through each sweep.
